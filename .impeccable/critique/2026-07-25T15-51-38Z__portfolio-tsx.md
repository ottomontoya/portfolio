### [P0] Two contrast failures that only exist in rendered pixels

What. Both independently reproduced by canvas readback, because computed styles cannot see through backdrop-filter or predict where an outline lands.

Nav inactive labels — dark/Skills 2.30:1, dark/About 2.86:1; light/Skills 4.11:1, light/About 4.25:1 (13px needs 4.5). Mobile mirrors it (2.33 / 2.46).
